# MacDevOps Conference Recipe and Philosophy

This is the recipe for an MDO-style conference: the ingredients, values, and design choices that make the event useful, welcoming, and worth coming back to.

The goal is not only to put known speakers on stage. The goal is to build a community of people who share what they are learning, help each other solve real problems, and leave with stronger connections than they arrived with.

## Core Ingredients

- [ ] Quick talks.
- [ ] New speakers.
- [ ] Diverse speakers.
- [ ] MacAdmins, IT, programmers, and security practitioners bridging communities.
- [ ] Talks about managing Macs and managing people.
- [ ] Big-picture talks that are not purely technical, but make people think.
- [ ] 5, 10, 15, and 30 minute talks.

## Topic Mix

- [ ] One day about GitOps.
- [ ] One day about security of CI/CD pipelines.
- [ ] One day about building things.
- [ ] One day about the security risks of the things we build, including MDM security, script safety, binary paths, and user-owned directories such as `/tmp` and `/private/tmp`.
- [ ] AI-assisted development: vibe coding, building pipelines, and then using security review to audit what was made.

> Do not wait. Tech debt today!

## Speaker Philosophy

- [ ] Do not build the event only around known speakers.
- [ ] Build the next generation of speakers.
- [ ] Encourage talks from people who solved one real problem.
- [ ] Help speakers shape talks with a clear story arc: start, problem, solution.
- [ ] Keep talks useful and focused instead of getting lost in every technical detail.
- [ ] Make the audience feel that they could stand up and share something too.

## Community Design

- [ ] Make space for the hallway track.
- [ ] Give attendees room to breathe, think, and talk with each other.
- [ ] Include many breaks.
- [ ] Keep socials on site when possible.
- [ ] Design the event as a place to build connections and find helpers.
- [ ] Be a helper.

## Food and Drinks

- [ ] Provide food and non-alcoholic drinks such as pop, juice, and water when the ticket budget or sponsors can support it.
- [ ] Avoid an unlimited open bar.
- [ ] Treat alcohol carefully and intentionally, for many reasons.

## Sponsor Philosophy

- [ ] Find sponsors who are already part of the community.
- [ ] Listen when attendees suggest partner companies they already work with and trust.
- [ ] Prefer companies that provide useful services and genuinely want to contribute to the conference.
- [ ] Discourage booth-first sponsorship.
- [ ] Encourage sponsors to send engineers, practitioners, and employees who want to talk with attendees.
- [ ] Prefer sponsors who mingle, discuss, and help instead of sitting behind a table.

## What Success Feels Like

A successful MDO conference feels like community building. It creates connections between MacAdmins, IT teams, programmers, security people, vendors, speakers, and first-time attendees. People should leave thinking, "I could share something next time," not just, "I watched the experts."
