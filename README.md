# MDO Conference How-To

This is the recipe for an MDO-style conference: the ingredients, values, and design choices that make the event useful, welcoming, and worth coming back to.

The goal is not only to put known speakers on stage. The goal is to build a community of people who share what they are learning, help each other solve real problems, and leave with stronger connections than they arrived with.
